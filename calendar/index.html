Omarchy Calendar on plan2.co

A personal desktop calendar, not a public service.

This page is the homepage of a private installation of Omarchy Calendar, an open-source calendar for the Omarchy Linux desktop that uses the Google Calendar API. Google requires every OAuth application to publish a homepage and a privacy policy, including applications with exactly one user.

Omarchy Calendar shows its owner's Google Calendar events in the desktop's calendar, reminds them before meetings, and lets them create, edit, delete and respond to events from the desktop. This installation runs on a personal laptop and connects to one Google account. It is used only by its owner. There is no website to sign up for, no service offered to anyone else, and no server that receives anyone's data.
